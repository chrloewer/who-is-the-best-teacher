## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Initial static single-page site (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, `teachers.js`).
- Retro early-social-network "Teacherbook" skin: blue header, boxy cards, Lucida Grande/Helvetica fonts.
- Start screen with the subtitle "Wer ist dein Lieblingslehrer?" and a "Los geht's!" button.
- 7 voting rounds with two teacher photos each, random left/right placement, and a progress bar.
- Rigged scoring: Octavian appears in rounds 2, 4, and 6 and receives the vote whenever he is shown.
- Winner screen that always declares Octavian, with a crown, trophy, canvas confetti, and a vote tally.
- Click feedback animations, round transitions, hover effects, and a responsive layout. Honors `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Teacher dataset for 10 AI-generated teacher photos.
- README, CHANGELOG, and `.gitignore`.
